Reflex Rush is a fast-paced, browser-based reaction-speed game built entirely with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no libraries, no frameworks, no dependencies beyond a Google Font. The premise is simple: a circle flashes on screen for a limited window of time. Tap it to score; miss it and you lose one of your three lives. The game ends when all lives are exhausted.

What makes Reflex Rush more than a basic click-speed test is its layered difficulty and scoring system. Circles appear at randomised positions across the arena, preventing muscle memory from taking over and keeping every round genuinely unpredictable. The display window starts at 1 000 ms and progressively narrows by 60 ms for every five successful hits, eventually reaching a minimum of 420 ms — a sub-half-second window that demands sharp, focused attention and near-instantaneous reflexes.

The scoring system rewards consistent accuracy through a combo multiplier. Chain three successful hits in a row to unlock a ×2 multiplier, and five consecutive hits to reach ×3. A single miss resets the chain entirely, so decision-making under pressure matters as much as raw reaction speed. Blue bonus circles, worth 2 base points, can yield 6 points in a single tap at max combo — creating high-risk, high-reward moments of tension.

Red bomb circles introduce a critical risk-management layer. Appearing at the same positions as regular targets, they must be avoided — letting a bomb expire is safe and even rewards a "SAFE!" float cue. Tapping one counts as a direct life penalty. At speed, distinguishing a bomb from a bonus in under 420 ms becomes the core challenge.

A real-time timer bar drains across the bottom of the arena for each circle, providing a visual pulse that ramps tension as it runs low — shifting from green to amber to red as time expires. Subtle Web Audio API tones provide tactile sound feedback for hits, bonuses, misses, and bomb interactions without needing any external audio assets. The personal best score is persisted in localStorage, surviving page reloads and browser restarts, so players always have a target to chase.

Visually, the game adopts a cyberpunk / retro-terminal aesthetic — Orbitron typeface, neon glows, CRT scanline overlays, and a phosphor-green grid background. Corner markers, neon shadows, and a flicker animation on the title contribute to an immersive, arcade-cabinet feel. The interface is fully responsive and optimised for both desktop cursor and mobile touch input.
